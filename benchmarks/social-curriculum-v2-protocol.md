# Social curriculum v2: factual transaction cost and genuine reciprocal shortage

The first candidate failed its inspected validation: all four useful trades
succeeded, costly-trade and unwanted-invitation refusals were 50%, useful joining
75%, and measured reciprocal aid 25%. Normal survival remained 100%. Its warm start
also confused transaction costs. The baseline exposed a task defect: a requesting
partner often retained its own stock, so received gifts were not usefully consumed.
The original models, source, traces and failed measurements remain preserved.

Version 2 retains the original thresholds, individual-only reward and unchanged
rules-3 authority. It adds 12 candidate inputs computed from the observer's own
needs/inventory, the volunteered transaction terms, projected own balances and
remembered own-cache access that joining/leaving could revoke. These are facts,
not a good/bad label or utility filter. Affordable harmful choices remain enabled.
The 64-unit GRU/shared candidate MLP is unchanged in form; its candidate width is
64 rather than 52. A registered v2 artifact/format distinguishes it from v1.

The corrected help setup acquires real opposite supplies and waits while each
participant maintains only the stocked need. Both then lack a genuinely needed
resource. Helping counts only after both parties actually consume useful received
units. The reciprocal baseline harvests its actual surplus, waits up to twelve
ticks for its proposal while healthy, and searches locally for its helper before
offering a return. Other partner policies remain free to refuse or move away.
Social episodes run 128 ticks to measure delayed consumption. Desired resource is
seeded independently of starting camp. No items, needs,
positions, policies or membership are edited through a debug/snapshot back door.

Use more varied local demonstrations and recurrent PPO, optionally initializing
from the retained v1 learned social warm start. Declare every initialization hash.
Keep older-snapshot/selfish/reciprocal partner mixtures independent of task/seed
parity. Preserve normal survival episodes and individual memory. Do not reward
refusal, giving, joining, departure or group counts directly.

Training remains below 45,000. Validation remains 50,000–50,015 (already inspected
in part, explicitly used for model selection). Sealed 60,000–60,031 stays unopened
until a single candidate passes validation and is frozen. Compare v1/v2 and the
baseline on the corrected scenarios, and separate partner mixtures, population
sizes and memory/relationship ablations. Each episode must conserve and replay,
including frozen neural decisions. A bounded C2 result still does not by itself
complete LW-008 or establish emergent society.
